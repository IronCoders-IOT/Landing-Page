Connecting water with those who need it most

AquaConecta is a smart platform with IoT technology that revolutionizes access to drinking water, connecting providers with communities without constant supply, allowing real-time monitoring of the quality and quantity of available water.

Why choose AquaConecta?

With AquaConecta, users can monitor the quality and quantity of water in their homes, while providers optimize their operations and improve traceability in distribution.

Real-time monitoring

Monitor the quantity and quality of water available in your home from any device with IoT sensors installed in your tanks.

Smart alerts

Receive automatic notifications when the water level is low or if the quality is compromised.

Route optimization

Providers can efficiently coordinate deliveries, prioritize critical areas, and optimize their routes with real-time data.

Inventory management

Precise control of available volume and planning of distribution based on real data for more equitable management.

Who We Want to Help

Our platform benefits both end users and water providers

For users

Take control of your water supply and improve your quality of life in areas where only 13.3% of households have continuous supply.

  • Real-time monitoring of the quantity and quality of water in your tanks
  • Automatic alerts when water is about to run out
  • Scheduling and tracking of water deliveries by tanker trucks
  • Information on water quality and prevention of health risks
  • Planning daily or weekly consumption for more efficient use

For providers

Optimize your operations and improve traceability in water distribution with our specialized IoT-based tools.

  • Intelligent planning of distribution routes based on real data
  • Remote monitoring of tank status in multiple homes
  • Automatic prioritization of critical areas with low supply
  • Management dashboard with real-time data for decision making
  • Available in two plans: basic (up to 500 homes) and advanced (up to 1,000 homes)

The water problem

In regions like Ica and Chincha, thousands of families face a serious crisis in access to drinking water

The current challenge

  • Only 13.3% of households have continuous supply for 24 hours
  • Lack of traceability and control per household in distribution
  • Risks in the quality of water distributed by tankers
  • Dependence on manual methods without real-time data
  • Health problems due to inadequate storage

Our solution

AquaConecta uses IoT sensors installed in domestic tanks to:

  • Monitor water levels and quality in real-time
  • Transmit data to a platform accessible via web and mobile
  • Allow users to visualize their consumption and receive alerts
  • Help providers optimize routes and prioritize critical areas
  • Reduce health risks and improve equitable distribution
